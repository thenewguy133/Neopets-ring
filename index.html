About The Two Ring

The Two Ring is one of the rarest cards ever printed for the Neopets Battledome Trading Card Game. Only two serialized copies exist, making it one of the rarest Neopets trading cards ever produced. Because of its rarity, I have created this page so owners searching for information—or people who discover one in the future—can easily find me through Google.
